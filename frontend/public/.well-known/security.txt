# Security Disclosure Policy for Smart AI Tutor

## Reporting Security Vulnerabilities

If you discover a security vulnerability in Smart AI Tutor, please report it to us at:
security@smart-ai-tutor.example.com

We take all security reports seriously and will respond within 48 hours.

## What to Include in Your Report

- Description of the vulnerability
- Steps to reproduce the issue
- Potential impact of the vulnerability
- Any proof-of-concept code or screenshots
- Your contact information for follow-up

## Our Commitment

- We will acknowledge receipt of your report within 48 hours
- We will provide regular updates on our progress
- We will credit reporters who follow responsible disclosure
- We will not pursue legal action against security researchers who:
  - Act in good faith
  - Do not access or modify user data
  - Do not disrupt service
  - Report findings privately

## Scope

In-scope vulnerabilities include:
- Authentication and authorization bypass
- SQL injection, XSS, CSRF
- Remote code execution
- Information disclosure
- Session hijacking
- Security misconfiguration

Out-of-scope:
- Denial of service attacks
- Social engineering
- Physical attacks
- Issues in third-party dependencies (report to maintainers)

## Timeline

- Initial response: Within 48 hours
- Vulnerability assessment: Within 1 week
- Fix deployment: Based on severity (Critical: <24hrs, High: <7 days, Medium: <30 days)
- Public disclosure: After fix is deployed (coordinated with reporter)

Thank you for helping keep Smart AI Tutor secure!
